# Surface: Rayana Social Hub app (all screens)

Mode: operate. Phone-first (Android, 390 px), also desktop. Audience and job: Oran checks this week first (what's due, what's ready for him, what to film), then saves references in seconds. The agent writes the same files. Nothing publishes.

Content and states: week slots on a 2-week cycle with A/B/C trial windows, posts with status (planned to posted), ideas, references (link, picture, video), platform guidelines, feed preview, pipeline board, trial results. Empty, loading, saving, error, read-only (view-only key) states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hub is the green A-frame chalkboard at the Rayana market stall: this week chalked up like the menu, today's special is what needs Oran, and the work is notes pinned under it. Refuses the cream page + serif title + identical card list that v1 and every AI planner ship.

OWN-WORLD: Committed palette. Deep green #1F3B31 is the board (screen heads, tab bar, rail) inside a brass #C0A06A frame; chalk cream #F1E8D8 lettering on it; sack ground under paper notes #F8F3EA; oxblood #5B2024 only for "needs Oran". Type: Cabin Sketch (chalk hand) only on the board for titles, day names and big counts; Libre Baskerville for sheet titles and guide headings; Plus Jakarta Sans for all working text. Notes sit pinned at a slight angle and straighten under the hand. 4 px controls, 6 px notes, soft offset shadows.

STORY: Oran opens the hub and reads the board: the week chalked day by day, today's special (what needs him), the market. Below, he taps a pinned note to check or change it, and saves a reference with the + button from any screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The board fills the top: brass frame, chalk "This week", the date range and cycle week, one chalked line per posting day (day, series or post, state), then "Today's special" in warm chalk naming what needs him. Below on the sack ground: pinned notes for what is ready, then the full week by day. Green bottom tab bar: Week, Pipeline, Feed, References, Guides. Oxblood + button above it.

FORM: Market Board (the green A-frame chalkboard at a market coffee stall), position 1 on my ordered list, chosen by Oran over the roll's Consignment. seed:b2d1bb952e1c730c. Raises kept: status as printed marks in a fixed cell (centre rail); one text size in lists, rank by weight (timetable); fixed series furniture, a short code (character roster); feed preview reflows only in whole cells (Crouwel). Signature interaction: pinned notes tilt and straighten on hover or press (Oran's request, from the decision page's side cards).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
